About us

關於我們

We are a B2B hardware trading company registered in Hong Kong. Our team has years of experience in cross-border IT hardware trade between Asia and Europe. We purchase from authorised distributors and verified suppliers in Hong Kong and mainland China, and deliver to our partners in Europe and other regions with full commercial documentation.

我們是一家在香港註冊的B2B硬件貿易公司，團隊在亞洲與歐洲之間的跨境IT硬件貿易方面擁有多年經驗。我們從香港及中國內地的授權分銷商和經核實的供應商採購，並以完整的商業文件向歐洲及其他地區的合作夥伴交付。
